Questions

Is it really my street?

Yes. The buildings, streets, parks, rivers, house numbers and shop names come from OpenStreetMap, the free world map made by volunteers. The hills are real too, from the open elevation data of Mapterhorn (drawn a little taller so you notice them). Building heights are guessed where the map has none, so your building might be a floor or two off.

Do I need an account?

No. Press Play and go: your game is saved in this browser. Create a free account to save it to the cloud, keep it across devices and appear on the leaderboard.

Who sees my address?

Nobody else. The address you type is looked up on OpenStreetMap's search to find it on the map. If you have an account, it's stored with your save so your world loads in the same place. Other players only see the name you choose.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in any modern browser. Drag on the left to walk, drag on the right to look. Phones get lighter graphics automatically.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads, no in-app purchases. All the money in the game is pretend money.

What do I need?

An internet connection (the map streams in as you explore) and a browser with WebGL, which every recent browser has.
